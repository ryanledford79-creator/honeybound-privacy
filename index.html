Privacy Policy — Honeybound

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Honeybound ("the game", "we") is a single-player mobile game. Your privacy matters, and this policy explains — in plain terms — what the game does and does not do with information.

The short version

Honeybound does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no logins, no ads, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

Information we collect

None. The game does not gather your name, email, location, contacts, device identifiers, or any other personal data.

Game save data

Your progress — levels cleared, bee career, field-guide discoveries, and audio settings — is saved only on your own device. It is never transmitted to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the game removes it.

Third parties

Honeybound contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or social SDKs, and does not send data to any external service.

Children

Honeybound is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date above.
